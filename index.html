Important guide notes

Accuracy note: This first app build organizes the current roadmap into an interactive offline-capable shell. Some encounter timing, DLC roster details, exact move compatibility and late-game item sources still require in-game/source verification. Where not confirmed, the table explicitly says to verify rather than invent a location or unlock.
  • Research Level 14 is not a mandatory Phase 1 target. Follow the tasks actually unlocked and available to you.
  • Only one Quick Claw gift is confirmed from the Vert Sector 2 stall NPC. Do not plan around receiving three.
  • Side Mission 20 gives Oran Berry ×10; Side Mission 21 gives one Wise Glasses; Side Mission 23 gives Soft Sand.
  • A Mega Stone occupies the held-item slot. A Pokémon cannot hold a Mega Stone and a second held item simultaneously.
  • Feraligatr's recommended Water attack is Liquidation/Waterfall when available; Waterfall is not assumed to be a level-up move.
  • Do not assume Dragon Dance is Dragonite's Z-A level-up move. Confirm moves in the in-game move menu.
